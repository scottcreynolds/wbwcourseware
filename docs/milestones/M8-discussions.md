# M8: Discussions

## Outcome

Cohort has simple topics and one-level replies.

## Scope

- Topic/reply schema and RLS.
- Create/read/edit/delete own content.
- Teacher moderation of all content.
- Soft deletion/moderation history.
- Accessible topic list and reply composer.

## Acceptance

- No nested replies.
- Authors cannot edit/delete others unless teacher.
- Cross-cohort and removed-user access denied.
- Deleted content hidden from ordinary view while record remains.
